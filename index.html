What is new in NeverMiss 2.0

New in 2.0: study timers, Pomodoro breaks and count-up sessions; optional strict commitments; app and supported feed blocking; daily limits and independent one-time or recurring app schedules; study tasks, a weekly timetable and local insights.

Custom study time, offline soundscapes, fullscreen lessons, app schedules, optional DND and clearer timer/reminder alerts. Read below for data handling and system limitations.

1. Your data stays on your phone

Your alarms, reminders, bills, settings and Focus records are saved on your device. They are not uploaded to a NeverMiss server. You do not need an account, and this version has no ads or developer analytics.

2. What Focus checks

With your permission, Focus checks which app is open and parts of supported app screens to block distractions. It can also check a website address. These checks happen on your phone; NeverMiss does not save screen content or a browsing history. App names, time spent in apps, study results and links you choose to save are kept locally for your rules and reports. App schedules and daily limits can work even when you are not studying.

3. When the internet is used

If you open a website or YouTube lesson, that service receives your request and network address and may save cookies or website data. YouTube is also contacted when checking approved channels. These services have their own privacy rules. The included nature sounds and other audio play offline.

4. Optional website filtering

If you enable DNS filtering, allowed website-name requests are sent to Cloudflare at 1.1.1.1. Cloudflare can see those names and your network address. NeverMiss does not keep a DNS history. This feature is not an encrypted privacy VPN and cannot block every kind of connection. It may replace another VPN on your phone.

5. You control permissions

Permissions are used to ring alarms, show alerts, block selected apps, scan QR challenges and silence notifications during study. We do not request microphone or contacts access. You can turn permissions off in Android settings. Strict mode is optional and has an emergency exit. Android still controls alarm delivery, DND exceptions and calls.

6. Delete or back up your data

You can delete saved items and clear Focus reports in the app. App-use reports default to 30 days and are cleaned up when reports are saved. Deleting reports does not reset active limits. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data. Copies you exported or backed up remain until you delete them separately. Android backups or a cloud folder you choose may store copies outside your phone. Exported JSON files are not encrypted; keep them somewhere private. Not every Focus setting is included in backups; independent app schedules stay on this device.

Email medicobhai@gmail.com or riturajprince@outlook.com. Your email service handles that message. Websites and YouTube have their own age requirements. We will update this policy when data handling changes.
