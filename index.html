NeverMiss - Alarms & Alerts

Privacy Policy • Updated June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the NeverMiss - Alarms & Alerts mobile application (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. NeverMiss is built to be a beautiful, minimalist, and private alarm, reminder, and bill-tracking app.

We take your privacy seriously. Please read this document carefully to understand how your information is managed.

1. No Collection of Personal Data

NeverMiss is a local-first application. Your private data stays entirely on your phone.

  • No Server Storage: We do not operate external database servers to log or upload your schedules. All habits, alarms, bills, and reminders reside strictly within your device's memory.
  • No Account Sign-Up: You are not required to create profiles, enter emails, verify phone numbers, or hook up social network credentials to use the App.
  • No Trackers or Analytics: The App does not contain third-party tracking scripts, advertiser tools, or usage-analytics kits. Your app behavior and usage frequencies remain invisible.

2. Local Device Storage

Any items you input into NeverMiss are saved locally in a sandboxed, private SQL database on your device (implemented via Android's native Room library):

  • Alarms: Time configurations, repeat flags, and sound labels.
  • Reminders & Tasks: Schedules, notes, flags, priorities, and check states.
  • Bills & Budgets: Transaction names, billing frequencies, and dates.

This database is secure and locked from other apps. Uninstalling the app permanently purges this local database unless backed up using standard system-wide Google Account backups.

3. Required Device Permissions

To deliver precise features, NeverMiss requests the following permissions from Android. We only use them to execute features locally on your device:

A. Exact Alarm Scheduling

Allows the App to trigger reminders at the exact millisecond configured by the user, overcoming standard background idle sleep delays.

B. Full-Screen Intents

Enables displaying the full wakeup call trigger screen even when your device is completely locked, allowing quick snoozes and dismissals.

C. Post Notifications

Allows adding alerts, progress status, and task reminder banners to your device’s notification tray.

D. Receive Boot Completed

Enables the App to restore and re-schedule all your active alarms and reminders automatically when your phone is turned back on or restarted.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the App collects absolutely no data, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and is safe to be used by all age brackets.
